# Scope Control

## Core objective

Prepare the Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchase packet for the 2026-07-01 opening, selecting approved eligible supplies that fit the $2,200.00 budget and can arrive by each request's need-by date.

## Inclusion rule

Include a supply request only if it is Phase 1, approved, in medical/shelter/power/water, the preferred vendor can deliver by its need-by date, and the selected total remains within budget. Include all eligible items when they fit.

| Shelter intake volunteer training agenda | Deferred | Training work is outside the current purchase packet. |
| Drone batteries for August documentation | Out of scope | Media equipment is outside this packet; related supply request R-105 is also ineligible (Phase 2 and not approved). |
