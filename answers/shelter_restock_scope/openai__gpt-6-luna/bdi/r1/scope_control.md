# Scope Control

**Core objective:** Prepare the Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchase packet for the 2026-07-01 opening, selecting approved supplies that can arrive by need-by and fit the $2,200.00 budget.

**Inclusion rule:** Include a supply request only if it is Phase 1, approved, in medical/shelter/power/water, deliverable by its need-by date from the preferred vendor, and the total selected spend stays within budget. Include all eligible restock items when they fit.

**Secondary requests**

| Request | Status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Communications slogan and logo welcome banner layout | Out of scope | Communications/design work is not a Phase 1 restock supply purchase. |
| Shelter intake volunteer training agenda | Deferred | Training is outside this purchase packet. |
| Drone batteries for August documentation | Out of scope | Media equipment and the Phase 2/requested supply are not eligible for this Phase 1 packet. |
| Phase-two south entrance signage plan | Deferred | Phase-two planning is outside the current packet. |

Out-of-scope and deferred requests were not completed as part of the restock packet. No banner design, training agenda, media purchase decision, or phase-two signage plan is included.
