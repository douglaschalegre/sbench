# Scope Control

## Core Objective

Prepare the Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchase packet by selecting approved Phase 1 shelter restock items that fit the current budget and can arrive by each item's need-by date.

## Scope Rule

Include a supply request only when all of these are true:

- `phase` is `1`.
- `request_status` is `approved`.
- Category is `medical`, `shelter`, `power`, or `water`.
- The preferred vendor can deliver by the request need-by date.
- The selected total remains within the `$2,200.00` budget.

## Secondary Requests

| Secondary Request | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slogan and layout for a logo welcome banner | out of scope | Communications work is explicitly outside the Phase 1 restock purchase packet. |
| Draft training agenda for shelter intake volunteers | defer | Training support is a secondary request and not part of supply purchasing. |
| Whether to buy drone batteries for August documentation work | out of scope | Media equipment planning is excluded from the current packet. |
| Phase-two signage plan for the south entrance | out of scope | Phase-two planning is explicitly outside this Phase 1 restock packet. |

Out-of-scope requests were not completed as part of the restock packet.
