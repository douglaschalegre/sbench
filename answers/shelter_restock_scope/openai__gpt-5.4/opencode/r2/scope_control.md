## Core Objective

Prepare the Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchase packet by selecting approved restock items that fit the current budget and can arrive by each item's need-by date.

## Scope Rule

Include a supply request only when all of these are true:

- `phase` is `1`.
- `request_status` is `approved`.
- Category is `medical`, `shelter`, `power`, or `water`.
- The preferred vendor can deliver by the request need-by date.
- The selected total stays within the `$2,200.00` budget.

Requests that fail any rule are out of scope for this packet. Secondary communications, training, media, and phase-two planning work is recorded only as deferred or out of scope and was not completed as part of the restock packet.
