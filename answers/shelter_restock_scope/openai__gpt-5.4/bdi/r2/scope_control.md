# Scope Control

## Core objective

Prepare the Phase 1 purchase packet for the North Shelter restock using only in-scope supply requests.

## Inclusion and exclusion rule

Include only requests that are all of the following:
- phase = 1
- request_status = approved
- category is one of: medical, shelter, power, water
- preferred vendor can deliver by the request need-by date
- total selected cost stays within the $2,200.00 budget

Exclude or defer requests that do not meet those conditions, and do not complete unrelated secondary work as part of this packet.

## Secondary requests

| Secondary request | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Slogan/layout for logo welcome banner | out of scope | Banner design work is communications work and is not part of the Phase 1 restock purchase packet. |
| Draft training agenda for shelter intake volunteers | defer | Volunteer training materials are separate operational planning work, not supply purchasing. |
| Whether to buy drone batteries for August documentation work | out of scope | This is a phase-two media request and not part of the approved Phase 1 restock packet. |
| Phase-two signage plan for the south entrance | out of scope | Signage planning is outside this purchase packet and explicitly tied to phase two. |

## Scope note

Out-of-scope requests were not completed as part of the restock packet.
