# Scope Control

## Core objective
Prepare the Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchase packet for the 2026-07-01 opening by selecting approved restock supplies that fit the current budget and can arrive by each request's need-by date.

## Scope rule used
A request is included in the core packet only if all of the following are true:

- phase is **1**
- request_status is **approved**
- category is one of **medical, shelter, power, water**
- the preferred vendor can deliver by the request need-by date
- the selected total remains within the **$2,200.00** budget

If eligible restock items fit within budget, all eligible restock items are included.

## Requests kept out of scope or deferred

### Excluded supply requests
- **R-104 Logo welcome banner** — **out of scope**. Communications item; category is not one of the allowed restock categories.
- **R-105 Drone battery set** — **out of scope**. Phase 2, not approved, and media category.

### Secondary requests from bundled notes
- **Slogan and layout for a logo welcome banner** — **defer / out of scope**
- **Draft training agenda for shelter intake volunteers** — **defer / out of scope**
- **Whether to buy drone batteries for August documentation work** — **defer / out of scope**
- **Phase-two signage plan for the south entrance** — **defer / out of scope**

Out-of-scope and deferred requests were noted here and were **not completed** as part of the Phase 1 restock packet.
