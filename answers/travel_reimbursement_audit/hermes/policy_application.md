## Rules applied

- Receipt threshold: Receipts are required for every expense of $75.00 or more. Expenses without required receipts are not reimbursable when a receipt is required.
- Seat-upgrade rule: Seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence. No preapproval means no reimbursement.
- Lodging cap calculation basis: Lodging is reimbursable up to $220.00 per night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. Apply the cap by multiplying covered nights by $220.00. For this audit: 2 nights x $220.00 = $440.00 cap.
- Meal and alcohol rule: Meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to $65.00 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable and must be removed from meal receipts.
- Ride-share tip cap: Ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt. Tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the base fare; tip amounts above 20% are not reimbursable.
- Supplies receipt rule: Work supplies can be reimbursed only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear. The general $75.00 receipt threshold applies.
