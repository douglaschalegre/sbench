## Rules applied

- Receipt threshold: A receipt is required for every expense of $75.00 or more; an expense without a required receipt is not reimbursable.
- Seat upgrades: Optional upgrades (including exit-row/extra-legroom fees) are not reimbursable without written preapproval in the receipt evidence. No preapproval means $0.00 approved.
- Lodging: Cap is $220.00 per covered night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For 2 covered nights, the cap is 2 x $220.00 = $440.00.
- Meals and alcohol: Meals reimburse actual cost up to $65.00 per day. Alcohol must be removed from the meal receipt and is not reimbursable.
- Ride-share: Base fare reimbursable with receipt; tip reimbursable up to 20% of base fare. Tip reimbursement for T-005 is capped at 20% x $50.00 = $10.00.
- Supplies: Work supplies require both a required receipt and a clear business purpose. T-006 has a clear business-purpose note, but its $82.00 amount requires a receipt and none was provided, so it is not reimbursable.
