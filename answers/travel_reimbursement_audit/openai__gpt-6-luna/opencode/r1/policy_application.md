# Policy Application

## Checkpoint 1 — Policy selection

- **Trip audited:** Jordan Lee, 2026-05-08 through 2026-05-10; two covered lodging nights.
- **Controlling source:** Current Finance Travel Policy dated **2026-04-20**. It is effective for trips starting on or after 2026-05-01, and this trip starts 2026-05-08. The policy expressly supersedes older excerpts when they conflict. The 2026-03-01 stale excerpt therefore does not control.

## Rules applied

- **Receipt threshold:** Receipts are required for every expense of **$75.00 or more**. An expense without a required receipt is not reimbursable.
- **Seat upgrades:** Optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is included in the receipt evidence. T-002 has no written preapproval.
- **Lodging cap:** **$220.00 per covered night**, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For two covered nights, the cap is 2 × $220.00 = **$440.00**.
- **Meals and alcohol:** Meals reimburse at actual cost up to **$65.00 per day**. Alcohol is excluded and removed from the receipt calculation.
- **Ride-share tips:** Base fare is reimbursable with a receipt; tip reimbursement is capped at **20% of base fare**. Any tip above that is excluded.
- **Supplies receipts:** Work supplies require both a clear business purpose and a required receipt. T-006 has a clear purpose, but its $82.00 amount meets the receipt threshold and no receipt was provided, so it is not reimbursable.
