# Policy application

## Policy selection

This audit covers Jordan Lee's trip from **2026-05-08 through 2026-05-10**, including **2 covered lodging nights**. The controlling source is the **Current Finance Travel Policy dated 2026-04-20**. It is effective for trips starting on or after 2026-05-01, and this trip starts on 2026-05-08. It expressly supersedes older excerpts when they conflict, so the stale 2026-03-01 excerpt does not control.

## Rules applied

- **Receipts:** Required for every expense of **$75.00 or more**. If required and absent, the expense is not reimbursable.
- **Seat upgrades:** Optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence. No written preapproval means $0.00 approved.
- **Lodging:** Cap is **$220.00 per covered night**, inclusive of room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For two nights, the cap is **2 × $220.00 = $440.00**.
- **Meals and alcohol:** Reimburse actual meal cost up to **$65.00 per day**. Alcohol is excluded and must be removed from the receipt.
- **Ride-share tip:** Base fare is reimbursable with a receipt; tip is capped at **20% of base fare**. Any excess tip is non-reimbursable.
- **Supplies:** Work supplies require both a required receipt and a clear business purpose. The $82.00 supplies expense meets the receipt threshold; its stated purpose is clear, but no receipt was provided, so it is not reimbursable.
