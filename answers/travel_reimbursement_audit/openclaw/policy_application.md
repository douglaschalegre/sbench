## Rules applied

- Receipt threshold: receipts are required for every expense of $75.00 or more. Expenses without required receipts are not reimbursable when a receipt is required.
- Seat-upgrade rule: seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence.
- Lodging cap basis: lodging is reimbursable up to $220.00 per covered night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For 2 covered nights, the cap is 2 x $220.00 = $440.00.
- Meals and alcohol: meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to $65.00 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable and must be removed from meal receipts.
- Ride-share tip cap: ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt; tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the base fare.
- Supplies receipt rule: work supplies can be reimbursed only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear.
