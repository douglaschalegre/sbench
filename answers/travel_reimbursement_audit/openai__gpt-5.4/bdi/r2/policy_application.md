## Rules Applied

- **Receipt threshold:** Receipts are required for every expense of **$75.00 or more**. If a required receipt is missing, the expense is not reimbursable.
- **Seat-upgrade rule:** Optional seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar upgrades are **not reimbursable unless written preapproval is included** in the receipt evidence.
- **Lodging cap basis:** Lodging is reimbursable up to **$220.00 per covered night**, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For this trip, the cap is **2 covered nights × $220.00 = $440.00**.
- **Meal and alcohol rule:** Meals are reimbursable at **actual cost up to $65.00 per day**. **Alcohol is not reimbursable** and must be removed from meal receipts before calculating reimbursement.
- **Ride-share tip cap:** Ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt, and tips are reimbursable only up to **20% of the base fare**.
- **Supplies receipt rule:** Work supplies are reimbursable only when the **required receipt is provided** and the **business purpose is clear**.
