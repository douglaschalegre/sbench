# Policy Application

- Applied policy: `current_policy.md`, effective for trips starting on or after 2026-04-20.
- Audit period: 2026-05-08 to 2026-05-10, so the current policy applies.
- Receipt threshold: expenses of $75.00 or more require a receipt.
- Airfare/seat upgrades: optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is included in receipt evidence.
- Lodging cap: total lodging reimbursement capped at $440.00.
- Meals: capped at $65.00 per day.
- Alcohol: not reimbursable and must be removed.
- Rideshare tips: reimbursable only up to 20%.
- Supplies: reimbursable only when required receipt is provided and business purpose is clear.
- Output amounts are rounded to two decimals.
