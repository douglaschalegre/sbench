## Rules applied for this audit
- **Receipt threshold:** Receipts are required for every expense of **$75.00 or more**. If a required receipt is missing, the expense is not reimbursable.
- **Seat-upgrade rule:** Seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are **not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence**. No preapproval means no reimbursement.
- **Lodging cap calculation basis:** Lodging is reimbursable up to **$220.00 per night**, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. The cap is applied by multiplying covered nights by $220.00. For this trip, **2 covered nights × $220.00 = $440.00** maximum lodging reimbursement.
- **Meal and alcohol rule:** Meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to **$65.00 per day**. **Alcohol is not reimbursable** and must be removed from meal receipts.
- **Ride-share tip cap:** Ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt, and **tips are reimbursable only up to 20% of the base fare**. Any tip above that cap is non-reimbursable.
- **Supplies receipt rule:** Work supplies can be reimbursed only when a **required receipt is provided** and the **business purpose is clear**.
