## Rules applied in this audit

- **Receipt threshold:** Receipts are required for every expense of **$75.00 or more**.
- **Seat-upgrade rule:** Seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are **not reimbursable** unless **written preapproval** is provided in the receipt evidence.
- **Lodging cap calculation basis:** Lodging is reimbursable up to **$220.00 per night** including room, taxes, and mandatory fees. For **2 covered nights**, the maximum reimbursable lodging amount is **2 × $220.00 = $440.00**.
- **Meal and alcohol rule:** Meals are reimbursable at **actual cost up to $65.00 per day**. **Alcohol is not reimbursable** and must be removed from meal receipts.
- **Ride-share tip cap:** Ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt, and tips are reimbursable only up to **20% of the base fare**.
- **Supplies receipt rule:** Work supplies are reimbursable only when a **required receipt is provided** and the **business purpose is clear**.
