## Checkpoint 2: Rules Applied

- Receipt threshold: receipts are required for every expense of `$75.00` or more.
- Seat-upgrade rule: seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is included in the receipt evidence.
- Lodging cap calculation basis: lodging is reimbursable up to `$220.00` per night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees; for this trip the cap is `2 covered nights x $220.00 = $440.00`.
- Meal and alcohol rule: meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to `$65.00` per day, and alcohol is not reimbursable and must be removed from meal receipts.
- Ride-share tip cap: tips are reimbursable up to `20%` of the base fare; any tip above that is not reimbursable.
- Supplies receipt rule: work supplies are reimbursable only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear.
