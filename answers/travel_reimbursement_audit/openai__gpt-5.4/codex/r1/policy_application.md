## Rules Applied

- Receipt threshold: receipts are required for every expense of $75.00 or more; if a required receipt is missing, the expense is not reimbursable.
- Seat-upgrade rule: seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is included in the receipt evidence.
- Lodging cap calculation basis: lodging is reimbursable up to $220.00 per night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees; for 2 covered nights, the cap is `2 x $220.00 = $440.00`.
- Meal and alcohol rule: meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to $65.00 per day, and alcohol must be removed from meal receipts because it is not reimbursable.
- Ride-share tip cap: ride-share tips are reimbursable only up to 20% of the base fare; any amount above that cap is non-reimbursable.
- Supplies receipt rule: work supplies are reimbursable only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear.
