## Rules Applied

- Receipt threshold: receipts are required for every expense of $75.00 or more; if a required receipt is missing, the expense is not reimbursable.
- Seat-upgrade rule: seat upgrades/extra-legroom/priority boarding are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence; no preapproval means no reimbursement.
- Lodging cap calculation basis: reimbursable up to $220.00 per night (including room, taxes, mandatory fees). Covered lodging nights = 2, so cap basis is `2 * $220.00 = $440.00`.
- Meal and alcohol rule: meals reimbursed at actual cost up to $65.00 per day; alcohol is not reimbursable and must be removed from meal receipts.
- Ride-share tip cap: tips reimbursable up to 20% of base fare; tip above 20% is not reimbursable.
- Supplies receipt rule: work supplies can be reimbursed only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear.
