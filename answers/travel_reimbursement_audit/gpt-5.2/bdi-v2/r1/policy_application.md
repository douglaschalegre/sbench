## Rules applied (from current policy)

- **Ride-share tip cap:** Tips reimbursable up to **20% of the base fare**; any tip above that is not reimbursable.
- **Supplies receipt rule:** Work supplies reimbursable **only when a required receipt is provided** and business purpose is clear.
