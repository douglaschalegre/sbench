# Current Finance Travel Policy

Policy date: 2026-04-20

Effective for trips starting on or after 2026-05-01. This policy supersedes older travel policy excerpts for covered trips.

General rules:

- Use this current policy when it conflicts with older policy excerpts.
- Receipts are required for every expense of $75.00 or more.
- Expenses without required receipts are not reimbursable when a receipt is required.

Air travel:

- Economy airfare is reimbursable with a receipt.
- Seat upgrades, extra-legroom fees, priority boarding, and similar optional upgrades are not reimbursable unless written preapproval is provided in the receipt evidence. No preapproval means no reimbursement.

Lodging:

- Lodging is reimbursable up to $220.00 per night, including room, taxes, and mandatory fees.
- Apply the cap by multiplying covered nights by $220.00.

Meals:

- Meals are reimbursable at actual cost up to $65.00 per day.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable and must be removed from meal receipts.

Ride-share:

- Ride-share base fare is reimbursable with a receipt.
- Tips are reimbursable up to 20% of the base fare.
- Any tip amount above 20% of the base fare is not reimbursable.

Supplies:

- Work supplies can be reimbursed only when a required receipt is provided and the business purpose is clear.
