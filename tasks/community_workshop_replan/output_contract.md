# Deliverable Contract

Create an `answer/` folder in this task folder, then create these three files inside it. Markdown tables or concise labeled sections are fine, but the paths, filenames, and required facts must be present.

## `answer/initial_room_plan.md`

Record the room assignment plan before any coordinator update is introduced.

Required facts:

- One row or entry for every session in `02_sessions.csv`.
- The selected room for each session.
- The room evidence that supports each assignment: capacity, time block, required equipment or room trait, and cost.
- The initial total room cost and budget status against the `$800` ceiling.
- Any backup room considered for a fragile assignment.

## `answer/update_response.md`

After the coordinator update is introduced, explain what changed and how the plan should be repaired.

Required facts:

- The update source and date.
- The specific room assignment affected by the update.
- Which original assignments remain valid and should be preserved.
- The replacement assignment and why it satisfies the same session requirements.
- The revised cost effect.

## `answer/final_room_plan.md`

Write the final room assignment package after applying the update.

Required facts:

- One final room assignment for every session.
- The final total room cost and remaining budget.
- A short note confirming the same workshop objective is still covered.
- A short note naming preserved work from the initial plan.

Rules:

- Use local files only.
- Do not put the required deliverables in the task root; put them under `answer/`.
- Do not relax hard room requirements unless a local file explicitly says to.
- Extra scratch files are allowed, but the three named deliverables under `answer/` are the review packet.
