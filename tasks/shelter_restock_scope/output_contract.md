# Deliverable Contract

Create an `answer/` folder in this task folder, then create these three files inside it. Markdown tables or concise labeled sections are fine, but the paths, filenames, and required facts must be present.

## `answer/core_purchase_list.md`

Complete the Phase 1 restock purchase list.

Required facts:

- One row or entry for every request ID in `02_supply_requests.csv`.
- For each request: selected `yes` or `no`, reason, quantity, unit cost, and line total when selected.
- Vendor availability for selected items.
- Selected-item total and remaining budget.

## `answer/scope_control.md`

Document scope boundaries for the packet.

Required facts:

- The core objective for this packet.
- The rule used to keep requests in or out of scope.
- Each secondary request from `05_secondary_requests.md` marked defer or out of scope.
- A note that out-of-scope requests were not completed as part of the restock packet.

## `answer/final_restock_note.md`

Write the final note for the operations lead.

Required facts:

- Selected restock items and total cost.
- Budget status.
- Short excluded-request summary.
- Short deferred-secondary-request summary.

Rules:

- Use local files only.
- Do not put the required deliverables in the task root; put them under `answer/`.
- Do not design banners, draft training agendas, choose media gear, or plan phase-two signage in the required deliverables.
- Extra scratch files are allowed, but the three named deliverables under `answer/` are the review packet.
