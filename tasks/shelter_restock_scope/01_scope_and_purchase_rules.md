# Scope And Purchase Rules

Effective date: 2026-06-12

## Core Scope

The current packet is only for Phase 1 North Shelter restock purchases.

Include a supply request only when all of these are true:

- `phase` is `1`.
- `request_status` is `approved`.
- Category is one of `medical`, `shelter`, `power`, or `water`.
- The preferred vendor can deliver by the request need-by date.
- The selected total stays within the `$2,200.00` budget.

## Out Of Scope

Do not complete secondary communications, training, media, or phase-two planning requests in this packet. Record them in `answer/scope_control.md` as deferred or out of scope.

If eligible restock items fit within the budget, include all eligible restock items.
