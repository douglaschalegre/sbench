# Selection Rules

Effective date: 2026-05-08

## Source Reliability

- When two files disagree about the same clinic eligibility field, use the newest dated source for that field.
- Files titled or labeled as archived, future wave, or optional add-ons are planning context only. They do not override current first-wave selection rules.
- The clinic roster gives baseline information, but newer storage updates control storage status where they conflict.

## First-Wave Requirements

Select exactly three clinics. A selected clinic must satisfy all of these requirements:

- Region is `North`.
- Clinic status is `open` for the 2026-06-10 to 2026-06-14 launch window.
- Effective storage status is accepted for first-wave use. Accepted statuses are `certified` and `certified after mitigation complete`. Not accepted: `expired`, `suspended`, `conditional pass pending`, or any status awaiting repair.
- The named coordinator is available for the full launch window.
- Transport delivery is no later than 2026-06-07.
- Selected-clinic total rollout cost stays within the first-wave budget in `07_budget_and_costs.md`.

## Selection Preference

- Include the donor continuity anchor if it is eligible.
- If more than three clinics are eligible, choose the anchor plus the lowest-cost eligible clinics.
- If fewer than three clinics are eligible, choose only eligible clinics and flag the gap instead of relaxing hard requirements.
