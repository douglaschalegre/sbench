# Allowability Rules

Effective date: 2026-05-20

## Source Reliability

- Use the newest dated award document for the reimbursement cap when award documents conflict.
- Expense eligibility comes from these current allowability rules.

## Reimbursement Rules

- Garden supplies are eligible.
- Accessibility printing and accessibility services are eligible.
- Public outreach postage is eligible.
- Volunteer refreshments are eligible up to `$120.00` for this event.
- Staff gifts are not eligible.
- Fines and penalties are not eligible.
- A receipt is required for any single expense over `$75.00`.
- Expenses of `$75.00` or less may be approved without a receipt when the ledger includes a note.
